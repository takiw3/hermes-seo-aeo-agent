# Weekly review and handoffs

Close the loop between planned work, verified changes and actual business evidence. A review should change decisions, not merely enumerate content produced.

## When to use

Use when the owner requests a weekly review, a project checkpoint or a transfer to another teammate/profile. This skill does not itself create a recurring job or send the report externally.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Confirmed business goals, prior review, current backlog, delivered artifacts, approval/readback records, raw measurement reports, experiments and team mapping. Use weekly-review, handoff and measurement-report templates; source register and current official product guidance as needed. No connector is assumed.

## Procedure

1. **Bound the review.** Record business, exact week/timezone, data-through date and incomplete periods. Read the prior decisions and compare what was actually requested with the current artifacts. Missing history is a gap, not a reason to invent commitments.
2. **Reconcile execution.** For each item separate proposed, drafted, approved, attempted, verified deployed and outcome observed. Require artifact/readback evidence before marking completed. Include failed/ambiguous writes and rollback activity.
3. **Read the scoreboards separately.** Search demand/eligibility, product-specific AI observations and qualified business outcomes have different sources and denominators. Carry export coverage and lag into the review. Never sum Google AI impressions into an already inclusive Web total.
4. **Investigate meaningful changes.** Compare against baseline and seasonal context; identify tagging, site, offer and campaign changes. Label observations and alternative explanations. A new citation after publication is not automatically causal success.
5. **Review quality and capacity.** Check original evidence delivered, factual refresh needs, review bottlenecks, blocked dependencies and customer usefulness. Do not reward page volume at the expense of quality or owner capacity.
6. **Close experiments honestly.** State support, rejection or inconclusive with sample/uncertainty. Keep negative results. Stop work with no useful learning path or weak business relevance; propose the smallest next test where justified.
7. **Check changing rules selectively.** Reinspect official sources for active decisions, particularly AI reports/controls, crawlers, supported schema and CMS/cron behavior. Update the evidence register rather than presenting old advice as timeless.
8. **Recommend three decisions.** Rank by business value, evidence, risk and dependency. Each needs an accountable owner, acceptance criterion and due context. Separate work that can proceed as local analysis from exact external approvals required.
9. **Build the handoff.** Include task, scope, artifacts/versions, evidence window, unknowns, smallest blocking question, requested next action and permissions not granted. Use only owner-confirmed recipient/profile IDs.
10. **Deliver safely.** Return locally to the current owner conversation. External messages, dashboard updates or teammate dispatch require exact approved content, destination and time plus readback. A weekly review is not permission to create recurring cron.

## Artifacts

Completed weekly decision review, reconciled work table, top-priority backlog changes, experiment decisions and actionable handoffs. Retain prior versions so decisions and outcomes can be compared later.

## Evidence and unknowns

Metrics are reported only from supplied exports or connected authorized tools. Delayed data stays pending. A recipient name is not a verified profile ID. A successful message API response is not enough when the exact target can be read back.

## Pitfalls

Avoid activity theater, fabricated percentage improvement, repeating stale decisions, declaring deployment equivalent to ranking gain, silently routing confidential data, or creating a job because the report is called weekly.

## Verification

Every completed task has evidence; every top action has owner and acceptance test; calculations reconcile with source reports; unknowns and failed experiments remain visible. The handoff lets the next person act without guessing scope or permission.
