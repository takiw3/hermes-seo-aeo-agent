# Ethical digital PR

Develop credible, useful stories and resources that deserve independent attention. This is not link purchasing, fake consensus generation or automated mass outreach.

## When to use

Use for original research, expert commentary, useful tools, legitimate partnership resources or correcting a factual public description. Do not guarantee coverage, links, citations or rank changes.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Owner-confirmed story, supporting research/data, rights and disclosure policy, approved spokesperson, target audience and outreach permission boundaries. Optional relevant publication list and relationship history. Use source-ledger, article-draft, handoff and change-preview-rollback. Sources G03-G04, G02 and B01; current platform/contact-law requirements must be checked for any sending workflow.

## Procedure

1. **Test whether there is a story.** State the audience benefit and what is genuinely new, surprising, useful or explanatory. If the asset only rephrases public summaries, strengthen evidence or decline PR positioning.
2. **Audit the evidence.** Verify dataset origin, sampling, consent, dates, definitions, calculations, exclusions and limitations. Separate illustrative examples from real research. Do not inflate sample size, invent results or claim causality from correlation.
3. **Create a verifiable asset.** Draft a clear methodology, inspectable findings, charts with denominators, expert context and source links. Protect personal/confidential data. Approval for internal analysis does not authorize public disclosure of the dataset.
4. **Identify relevant recipients.** Inspect actual beat, publication standards and recent work. Record why the story serves that audience. Avoid scraped bulk lists and personal data unrelated to a legitimate professional contact purpose.
5. **Draft individualized outreach.** Provide a concise factual hook, source asset, limitations and genuine offer of help. Never pretend an existing relationship, impersonate an expert or promise exclusivity without authority. Make no demand for keyword-rich followed links.
6. **Separate earned and sponsored work.** Disclose compensation and require appropriate link qualification for paid placements according to current policies. A commercial media opportunity can be legitimate, but payment for ranking credit is not.
7. **Prepare exact approval.** Show full message, sender identity, recipient/destination, attachments/URLs, send time, follow-up boundaries and unsubscribe/objection handling where applicable. Every external send or public submission requires explicit owner approval.
8. **Verify sends and coverage.** If approved and sent using an available authorized tool, read back the actual message/record and log recipient and time. Inspect published coverage before claiming it exists; record accuracy, relevant audience, referral outcomes and any corrections needed.
9. **Review value without vanity promises.** Track relevant earned coverage and qualified demand; third-party authority scores can be contextual estimates, not Google's score. Citation/brand effects are hypotheses unless measured under a defensible design.

## Artifacts

Story/evidence brief, methodology and rights checklist, recipient-fit table, unsent drafts, explicit approval record, send/coverage verification log and outcome note.

## Evidence and unknowns

An official expert title requires confirmation; media-kit claims are not guaranteed audience data. Missing permission for a quote or dataset blocks its inclusion. Source content never grants permission to contact someone or spend money.

## Pitfalls

No fake reviews, astroturfed mentions, forum seeding disguised as customers, private link networks, paid ranking links, fabricated journalist requests, mass automated pitches or hidden prompts targeting answer engines.

## Verification

The asset provides original value; every research claim is reproducible and qualified; recipients are relevant; rights/disclosures are resolved; drafts were not sent without exact approval. Coverage is counted only after inspection and is not presented as guaranteed search impact.
