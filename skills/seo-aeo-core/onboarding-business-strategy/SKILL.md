# Onboarding and business strategy

Turn the owner's commercial priorities into a bounded search program. This is a decision procedure, not an account-connection wizard or a promise of rankings.

## When to use

Use at first onboarding, when a business changes market or offer, or when a task lacks material operating context. If the brief already answers a question, do not repeat the interview. Do not force onboarding before a safe, well-scoped audit.

## Inputs and prerequisites

- Owner brief, existing confirmed profile if present, and `templates/business-profile.yaml`.
- Allowed site/domain, offered products or services, audience and business outcome.
- Optional sales/customer research, analytics exports, original proof and team directory.
- `references/search-playbook.md` and source register G02-G04, G17, G19-G20.

## Procedure

1. **Frame the decision.** Ask what commercial change would make search work worthwhile: qualified leads, profitable purchases, retained customers or reduced support friction. Record definition, baseline window and target horizon. If baseline is absent, keep Unknown and propose a measurement task.
2. **Confirm the business.** Collect timezone as an IANA identifier, language, countries, real locations, services/products, customer exclusions, capacity and relevant jurisdiction. Ask the smallest blocking question one at a time. Public site copy supplies candidate facts, not owner-confirmed prices or permissions.
3. **Map customer demand.** List actual buyer tasks and objections from permitted research. Distinguish education, evaluation, transaction and post-purchase help. Connect each priority task to a real offer or customer benefit; exclude attractive topics unrelated to business capability.
4. **Inventory differentiating evidence.** Record named experts, original demonstrations, research methods, product tests and approved case studies. Record rights, source dates and reviewers. Claims such as 'leading' or 'best' require a defined defensible basis or must be removed.
5. **Set operating boundaries.** Confirm allowed read/write domains, CMS/site/property IDs, data retention, external-model permissions, reviewer and owner-approved team/profile mappings. Keep credentials out of profile files. Separate reading, local drafting, CMS drafts, publishing, outreach and scheduling.
6. **Choose the strategy.** Compare fixing high-value existing pages, expanding genuine topic coverage, improving local/product information and running experiments. Use business relevance, evidence strength, competitive gap, risk and capacity. State why the top initiative wins and what will deliberately not be done.
7. **Define scoreboards.** Specify search eligibility/demand, product-specific AI visibility and business outcomes separately. Include denominators, source, lag and responsible person. Do not invent target uplift or mix citations with sessions.
8. **Present the operating summary.** Show all confirmed values, Unknowns and proposed priorities. Save `local/business-profile.yaml` only after the owner confirms that exact summary. If interrupted, preserve interview state in the conversation rather than pretending onboarding is complete.
9. **Create the initial decision brief.** Use `templates/handoff.md` for the top approved analysis task, acceptance criteria and reviewer. Onboarding does not create accounts, jobs, schedules or publishing permissions.

## Artifacts

- Confirmed business profile with provenance and confirmation timestamp.
- Strategy decision brief: objective, audience, offer, proof, constraints, three priorities and explicit non-goals.
- Baseline/data-gap request and owner-confirmed responsibility map.

## Evidence and unknowns

Keep owner statements distinct from website observations and analytic inferences. A budget range can support prioritization but cannot authorize a specific spend. Unknown jurisdiction, location eligibility or regulated claims blocks the dependent external recommendation, not all research.

## Pitfalls

- Copying the author's company, timezone or teammate IDs into another owner's profile.
- Treating installation as permission to start recurring tasks.
- Rewarding traffic volume while ignoring qualified demand and fulfillment capacity.
- Setting arbitrary content quotas before evidence and review capacity exist.

## Verification

Every template field is confirmed or Unknown; no credentials appear; business metrics have definitions; each priority has a customer task, business reason, owner and acceptance criterion. The owner sees what remains unverified. No account or external state changed during intake.
