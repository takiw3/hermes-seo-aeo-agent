# Local business and GBP SEO

Improve the accuracy and usefulness of genuine local business information. Eligibility and real-world representation come before listing expansion or location-page production.

## When to use

Use for local discovery, inconsistent business details, real new locations, service-area questions or GBP review. Do not create profiles or pages for nonexistent offices.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Owner-confirmed business name, real locations, service areas, hours, categories, local contacts, website, reviewer and location/GBP IDs if available. Request evidence of customer-facing operations and authorized management. Use local-business-facts, audit-report and change-preview-rollback templates. Sources G12-G13, G04 and G02; recheck current eligibility rules for the exact business type.

## Procedure

1. **Confirm eligibility and ownership.** Distinguish storefront, service-area, hybrid, practitioner and online-only models. Check the exact current rules before recommending a profile. A rented mailing address does not establish a real eligible office; don't assume coworking eligibility without staffing/signage/customer evidence.
2. **Create a location truth record.** Owner-confirm name as used in the real world, address visibility, service area, phone, categories, hours and appointment links. Keep sensitive residential addresses private when service-area rules require it.
3. **Inspect existing public and owner-provided views.** Compare GBP, website, important directories and actual business evidence. Record URLs/screenshots/time and distinguish public observations from fields unavailable without authorized account access.
4. **Diagnose accuracy before expansion.** Flag conflicting hours, wrong category, broken destination, duplicate profiles or obsolete services. Prefer correcting real information over stuffing names with keywords or opening duplicate listings.
5. **Assess local pages.** Each page needs real service capability and unique useful facts: staff, access, availability, relevant work, logistics or localized proof. Do not swap city names into identical pages that funnel to one generic destination.
6. **Evaluate customer trust.** Draft neutral review requests and truthful responses only when requested; no fake, purchased, coerced or selectively misleading reviews. Confirm applicable review and privacy policies before any outreach or reply. A complaint is not permission to disclose customer details.
7. **Explain visibility honestly.** Google documents relevance, distance and prominence. The owner can improve accurate relevance and useful proof but cannot purchase a better organic local ranking or eliminate distance effects.
8. **Prepare changes per location.** Show exact current/proposed fields, destination profile ID, evidence, time, risks and rollback. Owner approval is required before GBP edits, verification attempts, review replies, messages or website changes.
9. **Read back and monitor.** Verify the exact profile/page after an approved write; record pending review versus live change. Monitor available calls, directions, clicks and qualified outcomes without implying every interaction became a customer.

## Artifacts

One confirmed fact record per actual location; inconsistency table; location-page brief; exact profile/page change preview; local measurement note and reviewer handoff.

## Evidence and unknowns

GBP management access is optional for a public audit but required to inspect private fields or make changes. Maps visibility varies by searcher location and context. A single observation is not a universal map-pack rank. Unconfirmed eligibility blocks profile creation.

## Pitfalls

No fake locations, virtual-office shortcuts, duplicate category profiles, keyword-stuffed business names, city doorways, review gating or publication of a hidden residential address. Do not claim all service-area businesses should show their address.

## Verification

Eligibility rationale cites current rules; every recommended detail is owner-confirmed; location pages have distinct real value; each change is scoped by profile ID and has approval/readback. Unknowns are visible and no external message was sent without exact approval.
