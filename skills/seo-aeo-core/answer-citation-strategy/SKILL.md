# Answer and citation strategy

Make useful claims easy for people to understand and verify. AEO is treated as accountable content and discovery work, not a special syntax that forces models to cite a brand.

## When to use

Use when planning answer-rich content, diagnosing inaccurate AI descriptions, or improving a business's source material. Do not claim that a prompt panel represents universal AI visibility.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Confirmed business facts and expertise, representative customer questions, actual pages, inspected official sources, and any recorded AI answer/citation examples. Use content-brief, source-ledger, experiment-plan and crawler-policy-matrix templates. Read source register G01-G04, G07, G19-G20, B01-B02, O01, A01 and P01.

## Procedure

1. **Name the surface and task.** Distinguish Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Bing/Copilot, ChatGPT Search, Claude search and Perplexity, including mode, locale and date where known. Define whether the goal is factual accuracy, qualified referral demand, relevant citation or business evaluation.
2. **Inspect actual answers.** Preserve full response, question, account/mode, timestamp and cited URLs. Read the cited pages and identify which specific claims they support. Brand mention, clickable citation, recommendation and source correctness are separate observations.
3. **Verify eligibility before editorial theories.** Check retrievability, intended canonical, actual indexing evidence where available, snippet directives and the effective Google Search generative AI control. Evaluate vendor crawler roles independently. Enabling training crawlers is not a prerequisite for all search appearances.
4. **Find the evidence gap.** Does the owner's site actually state the needed fact? Is it current, scoped and supported? Prioritize direct documentation, original research, first-hand demonstrations and clear product/location facts over generic restatements.
5. **Design an answer for the reader.** Lead with the relevant answer when it helps, then include conditions, method, evidence, comparisons and exceptions. Give tables only when they clarify comparable attributes. Avoid forcing tiny chunks or a fixed answer length as an alleged ranking mechanism.
6. **Make attribution checkable.** Place citations near material claims, name the actual author/reviewer and explain methodology. Stable entity names, dates and definitions reduce ambiguity. Do not fabricate third-party endorsements or claim schema establishes trust.
7. **Review external corroboration ethically.** Identify legitimate industry publications or resources that could benefit from the original evidence. Draft outreach if requested; no fake mentions, planted forum recommendations, ranking-link purchases or model-targeted hidden instructions.
8. **Separate measurement lanes.** G19 documents Google generative-AI impressions, not a universal citation rank. Bing citation metrics and manual answer samples have different units. Referral analytics measures detected visits, not every answer exposure. Record gaps instead of filling them with estimates.
9. **Propose a falsifiable test.** Specify a small treatment, comparator, stable prompts/pages, repeats, outcome and confounders. Owner approval is required for exact external content/control changes. Preserve neutral and negative observations.

## Current documented boundaries

Google's current G02 guide says no special schema or llms.txt is required for its generative AI features and discourages manufactured fan-out pages. G20 now provides a separate inclusion control; Google-Extended is not the Google Search inclusion/ranking control. G19 supersedes the inference from older G01 that separate AI reporting never exists. Recheck product availability in the actual property before reporting access.

## Artifacts

Answer opportunity matrix with customer question, current source/claim, evidence gap, proposed original contribution, destination page, reviewer and success measure; citation accuracy log; one bounded experiment brief.

## Pitfalls

Do not equate citation with endorsement, training ingestion with search access, or correlation after edits with causation. Do not rewrite a useful site into repetitive FAQ blocks. No guaranteed inclusion, rank, revenue or answer wording.

## Verification

Every proposed answer serves a real task and has inspectable evidence; official requirements are distinguished from editorial hypotheses; observed citations are checked against their targets; all metric denominators and unknowns are explicit. Nothing is published or submitted without exact approval and readback.
