# Ecommerce and product SEO

Align useful product/category experiences with stable discoverability and accurate commercial facts. Catalog scale does not justify manufacturer-copy duplication or invented reviews.

## When to use

Use for category/product visibility, faceted URL growth, variant confusion, mismatched feeds/markup or stock-related content decisions. Checkout and merchandising changes need commercial-owner review.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Allowed storefront, catalog sample/export, variant relationships, categories, prices/currency, availability, shipping/returns, images/rights and business goals. Optional Merchant Center diagnostics, GSC/GA4 exports and feed snapshots. Use url-inventory, content-brief, source-ledger and change-preview-rollback. Sources G08-G10, G15-G16, G02 and G04; fetch current product-variant/merchant-listing guidance for actual implementation.

## Procedure

1. **Segment the catalog.** Sample top-value, new, variant-heavy, out-of-stock and long-tail products plus category/filter templates. Preserve sample logic and do not extrapolate every SKU defect from a single page.
2. **Map user task to page type.** Category pages help choose; product pages explain the actual item; buying guides resolve tradeoffs. Avoid making these interchangeable or forcing one generic guide to carry transactional intent.
3. **Audit stable URLs.** Review crawlable links, pagination, parameters, variant selection and canonical consistency. Distinct variant URLs must reflect actual behavior and follow current guidance. Fragment-only state is not a dependable distinct indexed product URL.
4. **Control faceted growth.** Separate genuinely useful indexed filter landing pages from redundant combinations and temporary sorting/session states. Plan crawl control and indexability separately; preserve customer filtering and product discovery.
5. **Verify commerce parity.** Compare displayed price/currency, stock, variant, structured data and any provided feed. Record effective time and source system; prices change. Identify ownership of synchronization, not just the place where a mismatch appeared.
6. **Build original product usefulness.** Include confirmed specifications, compatibility, honest limitations, original testing/media where available, comparison criteria and accurate fulfillment/return details. Do not claim hands-on testing without evidence or fabricate aggregate ratings.
7. **Handle lifecycle intentionally.** Temporary out-of-stock may need a useful retained page and alternatives; permanently removed products require a justified destination or genuine removal response. Do not redirect every unavailable item to a category regardless of relevance.
8. **Prepare template and feed proposals.** Show exact values, affected SKU/variant scope, sample tests, checkout/price risks and rollback. Merchant Center changes, feed submissions and CMS writes require explicit owner approval.
9. **Verify approved changes across layers.** Read back product page, variant selection, markup and permitted feed/diagnostics. Test key customer paths without placing orders. Observe indexing and qualified product outcomes separately from deployment success.

## Artifacts

Catalog issue matrix, URL/variant policy, price/stock parity table, differentiated category/product briefs, lifecycle disposition decisions and bounded change previews.

## Evidence and unknowns

No feed access means feed parity Unknown, not healthy. Public display does not prove backend inventory. Merchant Center and structured data may complement eligibility but do not guarantee placements. Do not fabricate SKU revenue to prioritize changes.

## Pitfalls

Avoid blanket variant canonical rules without understanding the catalog, noindexing pagination indiscriminately, infinite facet crawl spaces, stale offer markup, copied manufacturer descriptions and speculative product claims. Never change actual prices to fix a markup warning.

## Verification

Important products remain reachable; variants and canonicals agree with the chosen documented strategy; visible/markup/feed facts reconcile where inspected; purchase paths remain functional; approval and readback exist for external writes. Coverage, excluded SKUs and untested systems are disclosed.
