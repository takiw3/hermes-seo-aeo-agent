# SEO/AEO strategy for an owner-operated business

## Objective

Build durable discoverability and qualified demand by making the business's useful knowledge easy for customers and permitted search systems to find, understand and verify. The profile is an analyst, editorial partner and implementation planner. It is not a ranking-guarantee product, an autonomous publisher, or a universal AI-rank monitor.

## Business-first intake

Start with the blank `templates/business-profile.yaml`. Confirm customer segments, exclusions, services/products, markets, languages, real locations, evidence assets, commercial goals, capacity, reviewer and permissions. Unknown means unknown. Do not import the author's business, timezone, channels or team IDs. Website copy is evidence to confirm, not authority to spend, contact or publish.

## Three distinct scoreboards

1. **Search eligibility and demand:** important canonical pages, actual indexing evidence, GSC impressions/clicks, query/page intent and qualified organic landing traffic.
2. **Answer visibility:** Google generative-AI impressions where the property report exists, Bing citation metrics where available, and reproducible product-specific answer samples. Each keeps its own denominator, date, locale and limitations.
3. **Business outcomes:** verified leads, qualified opportunities, purchases, contribution margin and retention where the owner's systems measure them. Analytics attribution is not causal incrementality.

Newer official Google documentation matters. As checked on 2026-09-16 UTC, Google documents a separate generative-AI impressions report and inclusion control with an August 31, 2026 worldwide rollout note. Older advice that AI traffic is only measurable in an undifferentiated Web total is incomplete. Account access and metric availability still need verification. FAQ rich results were retired in 2026; FAQ markup is not an AEO shortcut. Exact sources and caveats are in the source register.

## Operating phases, not calendar promises

### Establish the baseline

Confirm allowed properties and data access. Sample the site by template and business value. Separate static HTML observations from rendered behavior, live index state and real-user performance. Freeze raw exports and record coverage. Produce a prioritized decision brief rather than a dump of crawler warnings.

### Fix the constraints

Resolve critical retrieval, canonical, indexability, rendering and user-path failures. Preview exact patches with tests and rollback. The owner approves each external write; production changes are verified by readback. Search processing may lag deployment.

### Develop useful coverage

Map customer tasks to canonical pages. Build topic clusters only where the business has expertise and a useful next step. Commission original evidence before copy. Create briefs, source-led drafts, contextual internal links and accurate entity/product information. Local and international expansions require real service capability and localization, not doorway page multiplication.

### Measure and iterate

Refresh inaccurate pages, investigate intent overlap before merging, and run controlled experiments where data supports them. Observe AI answer variation with fixed prompt panels rather than cherry-picked screenshots. Preserve negative and inconclusive results. Reduce low-value production when review capacity is the bottleneck.

## Approval and automation model

Local research, reports, briefs and drafts can proceed within the owner's brief. CMS writes, new WordPress future posts, external notifications, outreach, GSC controls and every cron mutation require exact owner-approved payload, destination and time. Approvals expire and are invalidated by payload changes. Readback must prove what happened; ambiguous writes stop for reconciliation instead of blind retry.

Editorial scheduling is optional and distinct from analysis cron. A WordPress future post is a real publishing commitment, not merely a local reminder. Native Hermes cron is opt-in, draft-only, self-contained and preferably local-delivery. Installation starts no recurring jobs. Publisher credentials are never granted to cron. Behavioral policies and approval settings do not provide OS/network isolation.

## Ethical differentiation

Earn attention with original research, practical tools, transparent expertise and accurate details. No fake reviews, paid ranking links, fabricated case studies, false citations, disguised sponsorship, hidden model instructions, mass unoriginal pages or guaranteed outcomes. Cite sources but add value beyond them. Human review is accountable editorial work, not a checkbox over scaled spam.

## Capability boundary

This package supplies focused procedures, templates and separately documented local helpers. Measurement supports owner-provided exports and authorized tools already connected in the runtime. It does not imply bundled GSC, GA4, GBP, Bing, or AI-answer API adapters. Missing connectors are disclosed. Skill platform declarations reflect portable Hermes-tool procedures and standard-library Python helper dependencies, not a claim that all operating systems were exercised; this authoring pass validated on Linux. Service terms, crawler behavior and reporting products change; revalidate relevant official sources before implementation.
