# One specialist in the owner's workforce

SEO/AEO works standalone or with a chief of staff. No teammates, Kanban board, bot or routing jobs are created during installation.

| Role | Owns | SEO/AEO contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Chief of staff | Priorities, resources and approvals | Evidence-backed search backlog and decision briefs |
| Marketing | Positioning, offer truth, brand and campaigns | Search demand, content briefs, drafts and brand-safe recommendations |
| Sales | Pipeline and lead quality | Intent-to-revenue feedback; no invented attribution |
| Support | Customer questions and policy | Redacted question themes for useful help content |
| Developer | Site code, deployment and telemetry | Exact proposed changes, tests and rollback criteria |
| Ads | Paid accounts and spend | Organic insights without touching paid-media settings |

Read actual profile IDs from the confirmed business profile, then verify them using `hermes profile list`. Never assume a display name is an installed ID. The owner can assign a task through their configured Hermes Kanban workflow; verify CLI help and permissions first. A task card is not approval to publish or deploy.

Use the [handoff template](../templates/handoff.md). Include status, objective, artifacts, evidence, facts versus assumptions, scope/coverage, acceptance checks, outstanding approvals and one next action. Redact customer-level and account secrets. If the teammate or routing mechanism is unavailable, give the owner the artifact; do not silently create infrastructure.
