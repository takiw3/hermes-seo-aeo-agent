# Changelog

Initial business-neutral SEO/AEO Hermes profile distribution.

- Owner-confirmed onboarding and customizable operating template.
- Focused SEO/AEO procedures and source-grounded content workflows.

No live model or CMS credentials are bundled. See [test evidence and limitations](docs/testing.md).
