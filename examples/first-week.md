# First-week operating sequence

These are copy-ready requests, not claims of work already completed. Replace placeholders with your approved information.

## 1. Establish the business and baseline

> My website is [URL]. My business sells [offer] to [customer] in [market]. Our main goal is [outcome]. Use the business template, ask only for blocking facts, and show me what you would save before saving it. Do not connect accounts or publish.

Output: reviewed operating profile, current evidence gaps and three priorities.

## 2. Audit before expanding content

> Audit up to 10 accessible pages on [origin]. Prioritize our [service/product] pages. Separate static HTML findings from checks needing Search Console or a rendered browser. Give exact fixes and acceptance checks. Do not modify the site.

Output: crawl report, coverage/skips, commercial impact hypotheses and developer change set. An inaccessible page remains unreviewed, never implicitly healthy.

## 3. Map demand to pages

> Here are approved search exports and our offer proof. Map queries to intent and existing pages. Identify one high-value refresh and one justified new page. Do not invent keyword volume, competitor metrics or results.

Output: intent map, keep/refresh/create decision and two content briefs.

## 4. Produce one excellent draft

> Draft the approved [page] brief using [source files] and [expert notes]. Include title, description, complete copy, original evidence, internal links, CTA and claim ledger. Mark anything requiring expert confirmation. Keep it local.

Output: complete reviewable page, not a content outline disguised as a draft.

## 5. Review and schedule deliberately

> Review the final article for accuracy, rights, useful answers, brand fit and unsupported claims. Prepare the WordPress scheduling payload for [exact date, time and IANA timezone]. Show local and UTC time. Give me the owner-operated preview, approval and submission steps. Do not run approval or submission.

Output: exact local payload plus preview. Only the owner can authorize and submit. Without WordPress, deliver a CMS-neutral handoff; no fake scheduling receipt.

## Weekly review

> Summarize verified changes, search/AI visibility observations, qualified-demand evidence and next priorities. Name which measurements are unavailable. Recommend one next action and whether a recurring draft-only review is worthwhile. Do not activate it.
